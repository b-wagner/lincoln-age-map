    Sources & methodology

    Snapshot: October 20, 2026. Colors summarize dated residential records, not every building or individual parcel boundaries.

    Area age is the median decade within approximately 600 m, using at least 20 records. Each 120 m grid square shows its most common decade. Historic markers are separate records and may overlap residential records; multi-phase buildings use their earliest date for color.

    Blank areas may contain commercial, institutional, or other properties without usable residential dates. The shapes are not official neighborhood boundaries. Assessor dates may be approximate.

    Public GIS sources: Assessor parcels, historic preservation, streets, and city limits.

    How to use the map

    Click or tap an area for details. Select a group of historic buildings to zoom in. Use Find a place to search buildings or streets; exact residential addresses are not included.

    On a touch screen, choose Move map to drag or pinch, then Done moving to resume page scrolling. On desktop, drag to pan and hold Ctrl or ⌘ while scrolling to zoom.

    Keyboard: focus the map, use arrows to pan, + / − to zoom, Home for citywide, and Enter to inspect the crosshair. Escape exits map movement.
